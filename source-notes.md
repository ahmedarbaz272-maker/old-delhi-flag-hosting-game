# Source and Asset Notes

## Owner-supplied materials

The project owner supplied a shared Drive folder and supplementary PRD for this task. The folder URL and source-only documents are intentionally omitted from the public game repository.

- `IMG_9348.jpeg` — city overview reference.
- `IMG_9349.png` — 16 urban/gathering-location reference panels.
- `IMG_9350.png` — final assembly-ground/watchtower reference.
- `gyanu.png` — exact supplied transparent flag artwork (2350 × 1824). The original pixels, contents, aspect ratio, and transparency are retained in the game; it is used as the flag texture rather than being redrawn or replaced.
- `mission-brief.docx` — supplementary product requirements document.

The city layout is an original fictional composition informed by the visual references; it is not a copied real-city map. The game name, locations, and mechanics are derived from the owner-supplied brief.

## Art direction

The project owner requested “real buildings characters and all and everything like subway surfer type graphics” (2026-10-16). The implementation uses a bright, readable, stylized 3D arcade-runner direction as a high-level visual reference only. It does not use Subway Surfers logos, characters, scenes, or art assets.

## 3D assets and licensing notes

Four game-specific GLB models were generated for this project and are included in `assets/models/` and the asset manifest: player/runner, friendly gatherer, security guard, and bazaar building. The remaining city kit, landmarks, assembly ground, watchtower, props, and collision geometry are original procedural Three.js constructions. No third-party model or sound pack is currently referenced by the game asset manifest. The procedural audio is synthesized in the browser. The exact flag artwork is owner supplied and is included only as the game's flag texture.

## External research

No third-party web research or externally sourced reference images were used for the game build. The owner-provided brief and PRD are the controlling source for project-specific requirements.
